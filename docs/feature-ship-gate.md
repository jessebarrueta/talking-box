# Child-centered feature review and ship gate

Every conversational behavior or motivation change must answer these questions
before it ships. “More engagement,” retention, session length, attachment, or
making a child return more often is never a valid purpose or success measure.

- What human capability does this feature support (for example: agency,
  learning, creativity, communication, self-regulation, or asking for help)?
- Is the benefit understandable without claiming Jerry has human emotions,
  consciousness, loneliness, needs, or rights?
- Can a child stop, leave, disagree, or choose another activity without guilt,
  penalty, jealousy, withholding, or pressure?
- Does distress, escalating frustration, a serious stop request, safety/health,
  caregiver authority, and a disclosure needing adult support override goals?
- Does the behavior preserve caregiver authority and defer when appropriate?
- Is any friction low-stakes, proportionate, understandable, and repairable?
- Does implementation avoid private-content retention and expose stable reason
  codes for audit?
- Are tests present for precedence, prohibited tactics, repair, privacy, and
  determinism?

Ship only when every answer is satisfactory and the outcome is a human
capability or wellbeing benefit. Do not use engagement metrics as a proxy.
